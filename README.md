About Me

I am an AI Engineer focused on building practical, production-oriented systems around Generative AI, Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Orchestration, and Machine Learning.

My work sits at the intersection of AI research and software engineering. I focus on taking an idea from model experimentation to an end-to-end application by designing the retrieval layer, integrating models, building APIs, evaluating system quality, and deploying usable interfaces.
